How a change flows (demo)

  1. Direct swap: vet picks one of their shifts + a colleague's shift → colleague accepts.
  2. Swap board: vet posts a shift (and dates they could work) → colleagues offer a shift or to cover → poster picks one offer.
  3. PTO: vet marks a shift as time off → manager approves → shift shows as ★ Available → any vet picks it up.
  4. Manager approves (conflict check: already working that day / double-booked across locations) → schedule updates.
  5. Front desk gets a checklist to move appointments in Cornerstone by hand and ticks each line.
